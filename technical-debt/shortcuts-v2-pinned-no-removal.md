# Shortcuts V2: home shortcuts can only be disabled, not deleted

**Priority:** Low
**Affected areas:** Shortcuts v2 home-screen shortcut management

## Problem

Home screen shortcuts cannot be removed programmatically — the app can only
enable and disable them. As a result:

- There is no way to re-enable a disabled shortcut from within the app.
- Disabled shortcuts are also blocked from being opened from the shortcuts list.

## Proposed resolution

Add the ability to re-enable a disabled shortcut from within the app, and allow
disabled shortcuts to still be opened from the shortcuts list.
